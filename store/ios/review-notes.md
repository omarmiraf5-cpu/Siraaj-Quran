# App Review notes

Paste this into App Store Connect → App Review Information → Notes.
Replace every PLACEHOLDER. Sign-in required: Yes. Put the teacher email and
password in the username and password boxes as well.

Do not send the sample logins from the README. Those are not this school.

---

MyDiiwaan is a portal for Qur'an schools. The school creates every account.
There is no public sign-up inside the app.

Review logins for MyDiiwaan Review School:

- Teacher: TEACHER_EMAIL / TEACHER_PASSWORD
- Parent: PARENT_EMAIL / PARENT_PASSWORD
- Admin: ADMIN_EMAIL / ADMIN_PASSWORD
- Student: on the sign-in screen tap Student, enter school code SCHOOL_CODE, tap the student's name, PIN STUDENT_PIN

Signing in with email and password does not use location. You can open every
role from anywhere. Please do that first.

Location is requested only when a teacher taps Sign in or Sign out on the
attendance card, to confirm they are at the school. It is not used in the
background, and parents and students are never asked. If you tap that button
you will see that you are not on the premises. That is the feature, not a
failed login. The rest of the teacher portal is available without it.

Delete account: menu → Account → Delete account. Please use a spare account
if you try deletion, not the teacher login above.
