# App Privacy questionnaire

Privacy Policy URL: https://www.mydiiwaan.com/privacy

These answers match `ios/App/App/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`. If you change one,
change the other.

**Do you or your third-party partners collect data from this app?** Yes.

**Do you track users?** No. Do not tick Tracking on any row. There is no
advertising and no analytics SDK.

Data is linked to the person (it belongs to their account). It is used for
**App Functionality** only.

| Data type | Used for | Linked to the person | Tracking |
|---|---|---|---|
| Name | App Functionality | Yes | No |
| Email address | App Functionality | Yes | No |
| Phone number | App Functionality | Yes | No |
| Precise location | App Functionality | Yes | No |
| Photos or videos | App Functionality | Yes | No |
| Audio data | App Functionality | Yes | No |
| User ID | App Functionality | Yes | No |
| Other user content (lessons, notes, messages, other files) | App Functionality | Yes | No |
| Other financial info (school fees owed and paid; no card numbers) | App Functionality | Yes | No |

What each row means:

- **Precise location.** Read only when a teacher taps Sign in or Sign out, to check they are at the school. The map pin is not stored. The distance from the school is. It is still collected, because the phone sends the reading to the server for that check. Not used in the background. Students and parents are never asked. Signing in to the app does not use it.
- **Photos or videos.** A photo of class work or a worksheet, if someone chooses one. The camera and photo library are not opened otherwise.
- **Audio data.** A Qa'idah lesson a teacher records for the school's students. Nothing is recorded unless they press Record.
- **Phone number.** Only if the school adds one for a staff member or a parent.
- **Other financial info.** Amounts and dates of school fees. No card or bank numbers.

Do not add Usage Data, Diagnostics, or Coarse Location. Do not add Device ID.
